# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents a Power BI dashboard designed to provide an executive view of retail sales performance.

The dashboard tracks key metrics including total sales, total orders, total customers, total products, year-to-date sales, monthly sales trends, running totals, and top-performing products.

## Business Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to help stakeholders monitor retail sales performance, compare current results against the previous year, identify monthly trends, and understand which products are contributing most to overall sales.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Sales YTD
- Year-on-Year Sales Growth
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Running Total Sales

## Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI overview
- Current-year vs previous-year sales comparison
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Running total sales by month
- Top 10 products by sales
- Sales and order trend comparison
- Interactive filters for date, category, and product

## Business Value

The dashboard gives stakeholders a single view of overall retail performance and helps them quickly identify growth trends, seasonal changes, top-performing products, and areas that may require further analysis.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modelling
